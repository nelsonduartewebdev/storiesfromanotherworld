Stories From Another World

Short videos about the story of Magic: The Gathering

Stories From Another World is a fan channel that tells the lore of Magic: The Gathering in short vertical videos: one character, one event, under a minute each.

The publishing tool

The channel owner makes each video with a personal tool that runs on their own computer. After the owner approves a finished video, the tool uploads it to the owner's TikTok account as a draft, using TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. The owner then opens TikTok, checks the draft and posts it themselves.

The tool is used only by the channel owner, for the channel owner's own TikTok account. It does not read, collect or post anything for any other TikTok user.
