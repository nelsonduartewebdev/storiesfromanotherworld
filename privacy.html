Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

This policy explains what the Stories From Another World publishing tool (“the tool”) does with data. In short: very little, and none of it leaves the owner's computer except to go to TikTok.

What we collect

When the channel owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool:

  • the account's open ID, display name and avatar (the user.info.basic permission), and
  • access and refresh tokens that let the tool upload videos to that account as drafts (the video.upload permission).

The tool does not collect data about any other TikTok user, viewers, followers or comments. This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

How it is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's own approved videos to the owner's TikTok drafts and to check that each upload finished.

Where it is stored

Tokens and account details are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared or sent to any third party other than TikTok.

How long it is kept, and deletion

Data is kept until the account is disconnected. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens and account details from the tool. You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (wording may vary by app version).

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is used only by the adult channel owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
